DATA QUALITY WARNING
------------------------------------------------------------
All 35 point-level Bugesera records in wpdx_enhanced.csv
are labeled 'Non-Functional', while the separate grid-level file
adm_analysis.csv sums to 136 functional / 0
non-functional water points for Bugesera. The two HDX source files
disagree on functional status for this district. Recommend flagging
this explicitly as a data limitation, and NOT relying solely on
wpdx_enhanced.csv's status labels as ground truth for model training
without further verification (e.g. against MININFRA records).

Note: 'monthly_rainfall' is left blank in the cleaned CSV -- per the
project design this is populated later via a separate CHIRPS merge
step, not derived from WPDx.
